Phone as wheel

Your phone is the wheel

Hold your phone in landscape with both hands and turn it. In WHEEL mode you sit in the kart: the horizon stays level, the steering wheel turns in your hands and every kerb comes through the haptics.

  • WHEEL mode

    Cockpit view and phone-as-steering-wheel control, with gas and brake pedals under your thumbs.

  • Joystick or tilt

    Prefer the classic way? Switch to an on-screen joystick or tilt steering in Settings.

  • Controller and keyboard

    Pair a Bluetooth controller (PlayStation, Xbox and MFi). On the Mac, drive with the keyboard or a controller, with rumble.

Driving you can feel

Built like a simulator

Real Karting runs its own vehicle physics, written from scratch for karts, with adjustable assists so you can learn first and then switch them off.

Rain

Rain races

Grip disappears, kerbs turn to ice and spray hides the kart ahead. At least one wet race in every class, with raindrops on your visor.

500Hz

Real physics

A tyre model with slip, weight transfer, slipstream and kerbs, simulated 500 times per second. Every class has its own weight, power and grip.

FAQ

Questions & answers

Is Real Karting free?

Yes. Real Karting is free to download and play, with no ads, no loot boxes and nothing to buy to win: everyone races the same kart, and the best driver wins.

Which devices can I play on?

iPhone, iPad and Mac (a native macOS version). An Android version is coming soon.

Can I race against other people online?

Yes. Tap ONLINE for a quick race, create your own room or join a race in progress, with real drivers and the AI filling the grid. It's free and needs no account. The career still plays offline.

How does the phone-as-steering-wheel control work?

Hold your phone in landscape with both hands and turn it like a steering wheel. The gas and brake pedals sit under your thumbs, and WHEEL mode gives you a cockpit view with a level horizon. You can switch to a joystick or tilt steering in Settings.

Can I play with a controller or a keyboard?

Yes. You can pair a Bluetooth controller (PlayStation, Xbox and MFi) on iPhone, iPad and Mac. On the Mac you can also drive with the keyboard.

Do I need an internet connection?

Not for the career: it plays completely offline and needs no account, and your progress is saved on your device. Only the optional online mode needs a connection.

Is it an arcade game or a simulator?

It's a simcade: the vehicle physics are real (tyres, weight transfer, slipstream, a different setup for each class), but the assists let anyone enjoy it from the first lap. Turn them off and drive like a pro.

Which karting classes are in the game?

Six real classes: Rental 4T, Cadet 60 cc, Junior 125, Senior 125, KZ with a six-speed gearbox and the 250 cc Superkart on big circuits.

Which languages is it in?

English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Italian.

Does Real Karting collect my data?

Very little, and never your real name or email. The game has no ads and no third-party analytics. For the leaderboards it gives you a random driver name (you can change it) and sends data from each race to check that laps are genuine; you can turn that off in Settings and delete your profile at any time. The details are in the privacy policy.
