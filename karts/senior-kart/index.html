Senior karts in real karting

Senior is the main adult class for direct-drive karts. Engines are 125 cc two-strokes, water-cooled and more powerful than the junior ones, in the range of 25 to 30 hp. Because there is no gearbox, the class is about carrying speed: smooth entries, early throttle and clean slipstream passes decide the race.

Many national and regional championships run senior classes with different engine families, so the exact rules, minimum weights and age limits depend on the championship. Senior is the usual stepping stone before gearbox karts.

The Senior kart in Real Karting

In the game the Senior class uses the fictional VORTEX S-125, with 30 hp. It is quick enough to need real braking discipline, and it is the most powerful kart in the game without a gearbox.

  • No gearbox yet, and no tyre temperature; both arrive with KZ.
  • A 20-kart grid, starting from the back. Previous step: Junior. Next: KZ.

Frequently asked questions

How fast is a senior kart?

Typically around 110 to 120 km/h on a kart circuit. Lap speed depends more on cornering than on top speed. In Real Karting the VORTEX S-125 reaches about 110 km/h.

How much power does a senior kart have?

Typically around 25 to 30 hp from a 125 cc water-cooled two-stroke, depending on the engine. The Real Karting VORTEX S-125 has 30 hp.

What is the difference between senior and KZ?

Both use 125 cc two-strokes, but a KZ kart has a six-speed sequential gearbox, a clutch lever and brakes on the front wheels as well, with considerably more power.
